Beekeeper Studio comes with a set of built-in themes, so you can make the app look the way you like. Every theme has a light and a dark version, and the app can switch between them automatically to match your operating system.

## Changing the Theme

Go to **View → Appearance** in the app menu to open the Appearance window.

## Mode

The **Mode** buttons control whether the app is light or dark:

- **Light** - always use the light version of the theme
- **Dark** - always use the dark version of the theme
- **Auto** - follow your operating system's light or dark setting (default)
